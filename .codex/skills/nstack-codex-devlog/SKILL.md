# NStack Codex Devlog

## Workflow

1. Collect today's commits, task reports, and important docs.
2. Summarize technical decisions and tradeoffs.
3. Link to relevant files, issues, and reports.
4. Save to `docs/devlog/YYYY-MM-DD.md` when requested.

## Style

- Korean by default.
- Do not include secrets or participant personal data.
